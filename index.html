How to play

Place the Databricks capabilities

  1. Read each architecture zone and its hint.
  2. Drag Databricks cards from the palette into the zone where they best fit.
  3. Click a card, then click a zone if you prefer not to drag.
  4. Use Check architecture to score your placement and highlight cards to revisit.
  5. Reveal suggested answer when your team is ready to compare approaches.
  6. Export team answer to save the board as JSON.
